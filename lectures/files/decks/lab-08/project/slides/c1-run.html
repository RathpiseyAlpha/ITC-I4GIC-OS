Step 1: two buyers at the same time

student@server: ~/oslab-work/lab8

$ BUY_DELAY=1 bash store/buy.sh "$buyer_a" > evidence/a.txt 2>&1 &

$ pid_a=$!

$ BUY_DELAY=1 bash store/buy.sh "$buyer_b" > evidence/b.txt 2>&1 &

$ pid_b=$!

$ wait "$pid_a"; rc_a=$?

$ wait "$pid_b"; rc_b=$?

A trailing & starts a command in the background, so the next line runs at once. $! is the PID of the last background command.

wait PID waits for that one command and $? then holds its exit status. BUY_DELAY=1 makes the payment take a second, so the two buyers overlap.
