What threads share, and what they do not

SHARED

Shared by all threads

Code, global variables, the heap, open files. A change made by one thread is visible to the others.

PRIVATE

Private to each thread

Its own stack with local variables, its registers and its place in the code.

TRADE-OFF

Why it matters

Sharing makes communication cheap, and it also lets two threads change one value at the same time.
