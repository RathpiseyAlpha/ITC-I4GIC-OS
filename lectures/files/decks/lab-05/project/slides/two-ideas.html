Completion is not protection

COMPLETION

Joining answers: is it finished?

pthread_join waits for one worker to finish. After the join, main may read what that worker wrote.

MUTUAL EXCLUSION

A mutex answers: who goes now?

A lock lets only one thread at a time touch a shared value. A join does not do this.

SCOPE

This lab

Each worker writes its own field, so no lock is needed. Joining is the core. Locks come in a later lab.
