Looking at threads from outside

student@server: ~/oslab-work/lab5

$ ./threads/two_workers & pid=$!

$ ps -L -p "$pid" -o pid,lwp,nlwp,comm

$ wait "$pid"

$ ps -eo pid,ppid,comm | head -n 20

ps -L lists each thread: LWP is the thread's ID, and NLWP counts the threads of the process.

Raise the worker delay to about two seconds per step, so you have time to look.

Kernel workers may be hidden inside a container or WSL. Not seeing them does not prove they are absent. Observe them only; never signal them.
