What cron gives a script

FieldRangeExample
Minute0 to 59*/5 is every five minutes
Hour0 to 232 is 02:00
Day of month1 to 31*
Month1 to 12*
Day of week0 to 71 is Monday

A cron line has five time fields and a command. There is no year, so a date entry comes back every year unless something guards or removes it.

Cron starts the job in your home folder, with a small environment. Your shell's PATH and settings are not there.

Use absolute paths, and let the script find its own folder.
