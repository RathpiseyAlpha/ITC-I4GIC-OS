Your values: a name and a number

The last command prints them

example: your values will be different

$ oslab values lab10

Your personal values for lab10 (your-account):

  keep     = 3

  name     = daily

What they mean

  • name is the start of the name of every archive your script makes.
  • keep is how many completed archives stay.
  • Both are already written at the top of backup.sh. Write them down.
