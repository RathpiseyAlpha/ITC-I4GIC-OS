Key takeaways

01

Archive, then compress

tar joins the files. gzip shrinks the bytes. -C keeps the paths inside the archive stable.

02

Restore to prove it

A backup is a backup when you have extracted it and compared the bytes.

03

Keep the scope narrow

Retention touches only the names this script made, in your own folder.

04

cron is a bare shell

Absolute paths, no assumptions about your folder, and test with env -i first.
