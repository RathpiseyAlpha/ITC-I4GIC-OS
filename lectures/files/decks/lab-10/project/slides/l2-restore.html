Step 2: restore the new archive, and save the proof

student@server: ~/oslab-work/lab10

$ archive=$(find backups -maxdepth 1 -type f -name 'backup-*.tar.gz' | sort | tail -n 1)

$ mkdir -p restore-backup

$ tar -tzf "$archive" | tee evidence/restore.txt

$ tar -xzf "$archive" -C restore-backup

$ cmp -- project/report.txt restore-backup/project/report.txt

$ printf 'report comparison=%s\n' "$?" | tee -a evidence/restore.txt

The first line finds the newest completed archive: sort puts the names in order, and tail -n 1 takes the last.

List it first, then extract and compare. Do the same for config.ini. tee saves a copy as evidence.
