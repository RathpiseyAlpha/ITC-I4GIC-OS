Retention: keep three, touch nothing else

RULE

The policy

Keep the newest three completed archives that the script made. Remove older ones.

SCOPE

Narrow on purpose

Look only in your own backups/ folder. Consider only names like backup-*.tar.gz. Leave every other file alone.

HOW

A safe deletion

Sort the names, skip the first three, and remove the rest. Quote every path. Never write rm $(ls ...).
