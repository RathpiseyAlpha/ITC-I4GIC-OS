ACLs and special bits

ACL

ACLs

Extra rules for named users or groups, on top of the mode. getfacl shows them and setfacl changes them.

SPECIAL BITS

setuid and setgid

A program runs with its owner's or group's rights, not the caller's. On a directory, setgid makes new files inherit the group.

STICKY

Sticky bit

In a shared folder such as /tmp, only a file's owner may remove it, even if others can write the folder.

ls -l shows s and t in place of x, and chmod can set them with a fourth octal digit, for example 2755 or 1777.
