Step 4: no tools? Use a labelled trace

student@server: ~/oslab-work/lab11

$ cat > evidence/filesystem.txt <<'TRACE'

$ Illustrative ext4 header excerpt; not measured on this machine.

$ Filesystem UUID: 11111111-2222-3333-4444-555555555555

$ Block count: 32768

$ Free blocks: 25830

$ Block size: 1024

$ TRACE

Save the excerpt as it is, with its label. In your report write: metadata interpretation only; formatting not executed.

The numbers describe a 32 MiB filesystem with some space used or reserved. They say nothing about your real image or free space.
