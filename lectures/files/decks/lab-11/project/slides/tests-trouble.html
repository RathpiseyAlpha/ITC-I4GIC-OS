If something does not work

A large image and reset

oslab reset refuses an archive over 2 MB. Save your evidence and remove your own unmounted image files first, or keep the workspace.

No tools

Without mkfs.ext4 or dumpe2fs, the sparse-file work and the checkpoint still work. Use the labelled trace.

Stay safe

Only regular files in your workspace. See man du and man mkfs.ext4.
