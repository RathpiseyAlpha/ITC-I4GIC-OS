Two ways to deal with a deadlock

BEFORE IT HAPPENS

Prevention: a global lock order

Every worker takes Alpha before Beta. A worker that waits for Alpha holds nothing yet, so no circle can form.

AFTER IT HAPPENS

Recovery: a timeout

A worker that waits too long gives up and releases what it holds. The others can then go on.

IN PRACTICE

Use both

Ordering stops this circle. Timeouts also bound waits that come from other causes. Neither gives fairness.
