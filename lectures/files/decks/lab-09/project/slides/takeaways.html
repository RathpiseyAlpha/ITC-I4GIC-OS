Key takeaways

01

Four conditions

Mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption and circular wait. Break one.

02

Draw the edges

Holdings and requests that close a circle are a deadlock.

03

Order prevents, timeout recovers

One global lock order stops this circle. A timeout only ends a wait that has started.

04

Keep every wait bounded

Even with an order, a limit stops a hidden problem from hanging for ever.
