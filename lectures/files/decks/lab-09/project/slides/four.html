Four conditions for a deadlock

01

Mutual exclusion

A resource can be used by only one worker at a time.

02

Hold and wait

A worker holds one resource while it asks for another.

03

No preemption

A resource cannot be taken away. Its holder must release it.

04

Circular wait

Worker A waits for B, and B waits for A: the waits form a circle.

All four must hold together. Removing any one of them prevents a deadlock. This lab removes the fourth.
