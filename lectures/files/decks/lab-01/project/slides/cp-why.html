Why is there a checkpoint?

It shows what you can do alone

For most of the lab you may use hints and AI tools. The checkpoint is the one moment without them. It tells you, and the instructor, which commands you really know.

Your numbers are new

The names and numbers change at the checkpoint. An answer copied from a neighbour, or from your own earlier output, does not fit.

The instructor sees who needs help

Your answers arrive at once. The instructor can explain a common mistake before the next lab builds on this one.

It is fair

Everyone gets the same kind of task. You can take it even if an earlier task is not finished.
