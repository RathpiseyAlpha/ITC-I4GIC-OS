Key takeaways

01

Kernel and distribution

Linux is the kernel. Ubuntu is the kernel plus many other programs.

02

Program and process

A program is a file. A process is a running copy of it.

03

Remove and purge

Remove keeps the settings in /etc. Purge deletes them.

04

One arrow or two

> replaces a file. >> adds to its end.
