The ~ symbol is your home directory

sysadmin@localhost: ~

sysadmin@localhost:~$ pwd

/home/sysadmin

sysadmin@localhost:~$ cd /etc

sysadmin@localhost:/etc$ pwd

/etc

sysadmin@localhost:/etc$ cd ~

sysadmin@localhost:~$ pwd

/home/sysadmin

The ~ symbol is shorthand for the user's home directory.

Watch the prompt: it shows ~ while you are at home, and the real path when you are somewhere else.

cd ~ brings you home from anywhere.
