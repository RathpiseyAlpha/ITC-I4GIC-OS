Links and the helper

Internal links are fine

The helper accepts symbolic links that stay inside the managed folder.

Escaping links are refused

It refuses links that point outside it. Do not link to /etc, other homes or unrelated folders.

oslab clean lab3

Removes only the managed folder. Internal links go as entries, and external links are refused.

File exists means: look at the destination first. A dangling link can exist even when its target does not. Hard links cannot normally cross filesystems.
