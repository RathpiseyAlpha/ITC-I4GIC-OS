Your values: a name, a tag and two numbers

The last command prints them

example: your values will be different

$ oslab values lab3

Your personal values for lab3 (your-account):

  name     = maple

  tag      = old

  v1       = 417

  v2       = 463

What they are

  • name is your report, for example maple.txt.
  • tag is the word you add when you rename it: maple-old.txt.
  • v1 is the version number inside your report. v2 is the number in the new file.
  • Your values are different from the example, and from your neighbour's.
