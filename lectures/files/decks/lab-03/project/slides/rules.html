How you work

Core, Plus, Challenge

Tasks 1 and 2 are the core, for everyone. Plus and Challenge are optional, for students who finish early. Full marks need only the core and the checkpoint.

AI tools

Allowed in the tasks, Plus and Challenge, so test what an AI tells you. Not allowed in the prediction and the checkpoint.

Your own account

Use only your own files and processes. No task in the core needs sudo, and privileged or boot work is never done on the shared server.

Your first prediction is not marked for being right. A reasoned attempt and an evidence-based correction earn credit.
