Six helper commands, and why you use them

CommandWhat it does
oslab values lab4Shows your status word and the other word. Use them everywhere in this lab
oslab predict lab4Saves your prediction once, before the experiment
oslab hint lab4 1A hint, levels 1 to 3. Hints cost no points
oslab check lab4Runs your report.sh on test files and shows your milestones
oslab checkpoint lab4Opens when the instructor says so, near the end
oslab debrief lab4After the lab: you give the right answers to your prediction questions

Before the lab, run oslab doctor and oslab prelab lab4. It takes about ten minutes.
