Job control

student@server: ~/oslab-work/lab4

$ sleep 60 &

$ jobs -l

$ fg %1

$ # press Ctrl+Z to pause it

$ bg %1

$ nohup sleep 60 &

A trailing & runs a command in the background. jobs -l lists your jobs with their PIDs.

fg %1 brings job 1 to the front. Ctrl+Z pauses the front job, and bg %1 lets it run in the background.

nohup keeps a command running after the terminal closes. Use these only on your own, short commands.
