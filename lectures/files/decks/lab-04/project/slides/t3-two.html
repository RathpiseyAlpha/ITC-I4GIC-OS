Step 2: write your own report.sh

What report.sh must do

  • Take one CSV path as its argument.
  • Select every record whose first field is exactly ok.
  • Sum field 2 of those records.
  • Print ok total: NUMBER once.
  • Reject a missing input, and print zero when no row matches.

YOUR TURN

You write it

Nobody gives you the script. Decide where selection, field extraction and summing belong. A staged pipeline or one carefully limited awk program both work.
