$ sleep 30 & pid=$!

$ ps -o pid,ppid,stat,comm -p "$pid"

$ kill -STOP "$pid"

$ kill -CONT "$pid"

$ kill -TERM "$pid"

$ wait "$pid"; echo "status=$?"

$! is the PID of the last background job. A stopped process shows state T. A wait status above 128 means a signal ended it.
