How you work

Core, Plus, Challenge

Core is for everyone. Plus and Challenge are for students who finish early. Full marks need only Core and the checkpoint.

AI tools

Allowed in Core, Plus and Challenge, so test what they tell you. Not allowed in the prediction and the checkpoint.

Core 2

Nobody gives you the commands there. You write every path yourself. Hints cost no points.

A wrong prediction costs nothing. It is marked for being made and corrected, not for being right.
