🎯 Learning objectives — what you should be able to do (click to minimize)
  • State the rule: many readers may read together, but a writer needs the data alone.
  • Trace read_count, mutex and rw_mutex in the readers-preference solution.
  • Show a sequence of arrivals that starves a writer under readers-preference, and one that starves readers under writers-preference.
  • Explain how a fair (first-come, first-served) policy bounds the waiting time.
