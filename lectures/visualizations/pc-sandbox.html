Week 8 · Chapter 8 — drag wait() / signal() into different places and see what breaks: data loss, garbage reads, races, or deadlock
🧩 How to use the sandbox — click to minimize
  • Each process's body is a list of statements. Drag to reorder them, drop wait()/signal() from the palette, or delete a line.
  • Turn semaphores on/off and set their initial values. Set the buffer size and how many items each side handles.
  • Then interleave the two processes: click Step Producer or Step Consumer to choose who runs next — or let it auto-run a random schedule.
  • The engine reports the outcome live: correct, data lost / garbage, or deadlock. Try the presets, then break them.
