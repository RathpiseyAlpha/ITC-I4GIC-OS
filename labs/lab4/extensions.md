Complete these only after the [120-minute core](lab4-instruction.md). Work independently on owned files and record a prediction, a normal and an edge test, and a short explanation. These tasks do not alter the required rubric.

2. Run a bounded owned child process and inspect its parent/child relationship before and after exit. Do not manufacture persistent zombies on the shared server.
