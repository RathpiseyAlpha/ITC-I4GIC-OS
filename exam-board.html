Scenarios in play

Each part has a pool of scenarios and every student gets one per part, with their own names and numbers on top. Switch a scenario off to keep it out of the draw (a broken one, or one that leaked). The pool, the mode and the seed can change only before the draw.

The draw

Before the draw this is a preview of what Open will write. Draw now writes it today, so you can look at every paper before the exam; Open then keeps it. Once the exam has been opened the draw is final.

    A preset fills in the lock and the flags below; press Save to apply it. Exam: leaving fullscreen locks until you give a code. Practice: the room stays locked the same way, but the student waits for a countdown instead of asking for a code.

    Exam lock

    Turning the lock off does not unlock students who are locked now: use “Unlock all” on the Exam tab.

    Exam room

    Hardening

    While it is on: students cannot SSH in from outside (the exam page still works), their shells have no network, each student has memory and process limits, and wall and write are off. Turn it on just before opening the exam and off after closing it.

    Student accounts

    Puts exactly the roster’s accounts in the class group and gives each one the gateway key, so the exam page can open their shell. Run it once after the roster changes, before the exam.
